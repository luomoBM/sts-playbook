Sort the Toys Playbook

A compact reference for the free browser tidy game Sort the Toys and its two siblings. Checked against the live site on 2026-11-17.

The flagship, compressed

Sort the Toys is a free matching puzzle: grab three identical toys to clear them from the shelves, bank full sets, and save your helpers for the messy levels. It runs in any browser on phone or desktop with nothing to install - the game lives here.

The three habits

The site's own tips post names them: scan one shelf completely before moving on, count a full trio before you drag anything, and spend helpers only once the board has stopped handing you sets. Its diagnosis: most levels are lost in the last thirty seconds, not the first.

The siblings

Shelf Sort is the landscape shop wall - drag three matching goods onto one shelf and clear every shelf before the clock runs out, with boosters for the dead boards. Taba Lapka - Sorting is the portrait, phone-first cubby collector: drag each squishy toy into a cubby with two matching ones and collect sets of three. The clean-up hub indexes all three as one genre.

The name check

Two different games are called Sort The Toys. One is a paid PC simulator on Steam where you shelf an entire toy store at your own pace; the other is the free browser game with the timer. Same tidying fantasy, nothing else in common - the comparison post untangles them before you buy the wrong one.

Sessions that fit real life

A shelf clear takes two or three minutes, so the games fit coffee breaks rather than evenings. Free, no account, no download: start at the homepage and let the three habits sharpen whichever loop hooks you.
